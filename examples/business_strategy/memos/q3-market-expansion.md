To sustain our 35% year-over-year growth rate, our core initiative for Q3 focuses on localized distribution partnerships across European enterprise markets.[^global-saas-index-2026]

Competitive pressure from [Acme Corp](/competitors/acme-corp.md) in domestic segments necessitates accelerating our international presence. Furthermore, target unit economics are supported by our [Customer Acquisition Cost](/metrics/customer-acquisition-cost.md) efficiency gains.

2. **Pricing Harmonization**: Implement currency hedging and local payment options outlined in [Pricing Tier Revision](/memos/pricing-tier-revision.md).
