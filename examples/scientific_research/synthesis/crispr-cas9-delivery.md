# Executive Synthesis

Therapeutic translation of CRISPR-Cas9 relies critically on efficient, tissue-specific delivery modalities that minimize immunogenicity and off-target cleavages.[^nature-crispr-review]

# Key Delivery Paradigms

1. **Lipid Nanoparticles (LNPs)**: Outlined in our [LNP Protocol](/methodologies/lipid-nanoparticles.md), LNPs provide transient Cas9 mRNA expression, drastically reducing off-target mutagenesis.
2. **Adeno-Associated Viruses (AAV)**: High transduction efficiency in post-mitotic tissues, evaluated in [Zhang Lab Benchmarks](/studies/zhang-lab-2023.md).

# Comparative Matrix

| Modality | Cargo Capacity | Immunogenicity | Expression Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LNP Encapsulation** | High (> 15 kb mRNA) | Low to Moderate | Transient (24-48h) |
| **AAV Vector** | Limited (< 4.7 kb) | High (Neutralizing Abs) | Long-term / Persistent |
