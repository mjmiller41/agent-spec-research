# Study Design

Murine and primate cohorts ($N = 48$) received single-dose intravenous infusions of ionizable lipid formulations formulated according to [LNP Methodology](/methodologies/lipid-nanoparticles.md).[^cell-zhang-2023]

# Findings

* Achieved **> 85% serum protein reduction** at day 14.
* Off-target cleavage across 50 candidate loci remained below 0.05% detection thresholds.
* Results contribute empirical support to the [CRISPR Delivery Compendium](/synthesis/crispr-cas9-delivery.md).
