# Biographical Summary

Neil Alden Armstrong (August 5, 1930 – August 25, 2012) was an American astronaut and the first person to walk on the Moon.[^nasa-bio-armstrong]

# Mission Command

Armstrong served as Commander of [Apollo 11](/events/apollo-11-launch.md), overseeing manual terminal descent when the automated guidance computer targeted a boulder-strewn crater.

# Post-Mission Career

Following Apollo 11, Armstrong served as Deputy Associate Administrator for Aeronautics at NASA and Professor of Aerospace Engineering at the University of Cincinnati.
