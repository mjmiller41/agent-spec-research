# Overview

Apollo 11 launched from Kennedy Space Center on July 16, 1969, carrying Mission Commander [Neil Armstrong](/figures/neil-armstrong.md), Command Module Pilot Michael Collins, and Lunar Module Pilot Buzz Aldrin.[^nasa-mission-report-1969]

# Launch Telemetry & Staging

The mission was propelled to orbit by the [Saturn V Stage 1](/telemetry/saturn-v-stage1.md) booster, generating 7.5 million pounds of thrust at liftoff.[^nasa-mission-report-1969]

# Significance

The successful landing on the Sea of Tranquility fulfilled national space exploration objectives and catalyzed decades of aerospace advancements.
