# Technical Overview

The S-IC stage was the first stage of the Saturn V rocket, powered by five Rocketdyne F-1 engines producing 7,500,000 lbf of thrust.[^boeing-sic-manual]

# Flight Sequence

* **Ignition**: Center engine ignited at T - 8.9 seconds, followed by outboard pairs.
* **Cutoff**: Center engine cutoff occurred at T + 135 seconds; outboard cutoff at T + 162 seconds.
* **Deployment**: Propelled the [Apollo 11 Mission](/events/apollo-11-launch.md) commanded by [Neil Armstrong](/figures/neil-armstrong.md) to an altitude of 68 kilometers.
