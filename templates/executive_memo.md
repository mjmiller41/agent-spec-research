# Executive Summary

This memorandum provides leadership with an analytical assessment and actionable recommendations regarding [strategic objectives](/strategy/annual-goals.md). Over the trailing quarter, core revenue grew by 18%, outpacing baseline forecasts.[^internal-q2-review]

# Problem Statement & Market Context

Market indicators show rising enterprise demand in international jurisdictions.[^industry-market-report] However, existing regional operational capacity poses scaling constraints.

# Options & Trade-Offs

| Strategic Option | Pros | Cons / Risks | Estimated Investment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Option A: Organic Expansion** | High operational control, preserved margins | Slower ramp time (6-9 months) | $1.2M USD |
| **Option B: Channel Partnership** | Rapid distribution, localized sales reach | Revenue sharing (15-20% cut) | $400K USD |
| **Option C: Targeted Acquisition** | Immediate market share and customer base | High integration friction | $8.5M USD |

# Recommendation

We recommend proceeding with **Option B** for Phase 1 to capture immediate demand, while laying groundwork for **Option A** in fiscal year 2027.

# Next Steps & Governance
